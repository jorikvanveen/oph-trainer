# Orpheus interview training

Practice for the Orpheus admission interview through quizzes based on the official syllabus.

## Language

**Orpheus interview**
The admission assessment for Orpheus, covering the knowledge and rules in its official interview preparation guide. Candidates must answer without consulting notes.
_Avoid_: Quiz, training session

**Syllabus**
The study material on the official Orpheus interview preparation website.
_Avoid_: External audio curriculum

**Question bank**
The collection of syllabus-derived questions available for quizzes.
_Avoid_: Syllabus, quiz

**Quiz**
A set of syllabus-derived questions presented for daily training. A quiz is not a full mock Orpheus interview and has no required duration.
_Avoid_: Training session, mock interview

**Completed quiz**
A quiz with a grade for every question in its batch. Only completed quizzes contribute to progress.
_Avoid_: Partial quiz, saved draft

**Abandoned quiz**
A quiz stopped before every question in its batch has a grade. Its results do not contribute to progress.
_Avoid_: Paused quiz, resumable quiz

**Question**
A prompt that asks the user for a short answer or an explanation about the syllabus.
_Avoid_: Flashcard

**Answer**
A user's response to a question.
_Avoid_: Reference answer

**Reference answer**
An authored expected response to a question, based on the syllabus.
_Avoid_: User answer, model explanation

**Grading criteria**
Authored rules for assessing an answer against the syllabus. They specify accepted short-answer variants or an explanation's required points, optional supporting details, and contradictions.
_Avoid_: Reference answer, grade

**Contradiction**
A false claim in an explanation that conflicts with the syllabus and makes the answer incorrect, even when it includes the required points. Rejecting that false claim is not a contradiction.
_Avoid_: Missing required point, wording difference

**Grade**
A correct or incorrect assessment of an answer. A user-corrected grade has the same meaning as any other grade.
_Avoid_: Score, partial credit

**Progress**
An account's history of correct and incorrect outcomes for syllabus questions, used to describe what its learner has practiced and what they struggle with.
_Avoid_: Answer archive, admission prediction

**Topic**
A subject area within the syllabus that groups related questions.
_Avoid_: Quiz

**Account**
A learner's saved practice history, identified by an account key and accessible across devices. Every learner has an account, whether newly created or returning.
_Avoid_: Guest account, registered account, profile, membership

**Account key**
A secret that identifies an account and is its sole login credential.
_Avoid_: Username, password, public account ID
